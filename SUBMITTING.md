# Submitting a Presentation or Poster

Finished decks and posters are collected in [iHuman-Lab/presentations](https://github.com/iHuman-Lab/presentations), one folder per person (`presentation/<your-name>/` or `poster/<your-name>/`). You submit with a normal pull request.

## Steps

1. **Fork this repo** (`iHuman-Lab/templates`) if you haven't already, and do your work there — see [presentation/README.md](presentation/README.md) or [poster/README.md](poster/README.md) for how to build your deck or poster.
2. **Fork [iHuman-Lab/presentations](https://github.com/iHuman-Lab/presentations)** as well.
3. In your fork of `presentations`, add a new folder at `presentation/<your-name>/` or `poster/<your-name>/` (use a short, filesystem-friendly name, e.g. `jane-doe`) and copy your finished folder's contents into it — everything in it (`template.qmd` renamed or not, `assets/`, and for posters, `_extensions/` too, so it renders standalone).
4. Commit and push, then **open a pull request** from your fork into `iHuman-Lab/presentations`.
5. The lab reviews and merges it.

If a folder with your name already exists (e.g. you're resubmitting), update that folder in your PR instead of creating a second one.
